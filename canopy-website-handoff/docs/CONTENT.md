## How it works

### Hero
- H1: **How it works.**
- Lead: One webinar a month, in three phases. Here's exactly what we do in each, and the small part that's yours.
- Meta: Webinars-as-a-service
- Rail "Chapters": T−3 wks **Fill the room** · T 0 **Run the room** · T+ **Work the recording** · Month 0 **The setup**

### 01 · Before (`#before`; scrubber "T−3 wks")
- H2: **Fill the room.**
- Intro: Every pre-webinar task, handled, so the right people register and actually show up.
- Your part: Pick the topic and speakers. Approve the copy. Send it to your audience.
- **Registration pages**: Branded and customized for each event.
- **Email sequences**: Confirmations and reminders that get registrants to attend.
- **Speaker logistics**: Scheduling, briefs and reminders for everyone presenting.
- **Promotional copy**: Emails and social posts, long and short, ready for your team to share.

### 02 · Live (`#live`; scrubber "T 0")
- H2: **Run the room.**
- Intro: A flawless production, so the people presenting can think about nothing but the content.
- Your part: **Give the talk.**
- **Production**: All audio and video, with a private backstage to fix speaker issues before anyone sees them.
- **Attendee experience**: Waiting room, chat, Q&A and polls, managed to keep people engaged.
- **Live streaming**: Public webinars can also stream live to LinkedIn and YouTube.

### 03 · After (`#after`; scrubber "T+")
- H2: **Work the recording.**
- Intro: One live hour becomes a lasting source of trust-building content, and a list of people worth following up with.
- Your part: Load the data into your CRM. Use what we hand you.
- **First-party data**: The attendee list and registration details, ready for your CRM and email platform.
- **Recording and transcript**: The full session, plus a searchable transcript.
- **Highlight clips**: Short cuts of the best moments, for every channel you use.
- **Follow-up emails**: Written for you, with links to the recording, transcript and highlights.
- **Newsletter content**: The webinar's ideas, rewritten for your newsletter or linked straight in.
- **Video library**: Every past webinar and highlight in one place.
- **Analytics report**: Who registered, who came and how they engaged.

### The setup (`#setup`; scrubber "Month 0")
- H2: **Before your first webinar.**
- Paragraph: A one-time setup builds the system every month runs on.
- 1 **Onboarding**: Your audience, goals and brand.
- 2 **Branded pages**: Landing and registration templates.
- 3 **Zoom Webinar**: Configured for your events.
- 4 **Email and reporting**: Workflows and reporting infrastructure.

- Starting soon · H2 **Ready to hand it off?** · 20 minutes · a call with Canopy · see pricing first (link) · Button: Join the call
