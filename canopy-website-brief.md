## 2. The math (new section)

**Label:** 01:00 The math

**Headline:** Most webinars return less than they could.

**Body:**

You already know a good webinar can bring in clients. Running one, though, involves a long list of small jobs: registration pages, reminder emails, speaker logistics, AV, recordings and follow-up. At most firms those jobs fall to people who already have full workloads, so webinars happen whenever someone finds the time.

Most of the lost value comes after the session. Follow-up emails go out late or not at all. Attendee lists stay in a Zoom export and never reach the CRM. The recording sits in a shared drive because no one had time to edit it.

**Comparison table:**

| Running it yourself | With Canopy |
| --- | --- |
| Webinars happen when someone has time | One webinar every month, on schedule |
| Senior people handle logistics and AV | Your team presents, then goes back to client work |

| Attendee list stays in a Zoom export | Attendee and registration data handed off for your CRM |
| The recording sits in a folder | Clips, a transcript, newsletter material and a video library |

**Closing line:** Canopy runs every stage of the webinar, so your firm gets more value from each one.

## 5. How it works

**Label:** 07:00 The run-of-show

**Headline:** The same process for every webinar.

**Body:** You'll always know what we need from you and by when.

1. **45–60 days out: kickoff.** Tell us the webinar is coming. We send a short brief covering purpose, definition of success, audience, key ideas and speakers.
2. **Drafts for your approval.** We write the registration page, promotional emails and social copy, plus a timeline with key dates.
3. **You promote, we prepare.** You line up speakers and drive registrations. We run reminders, speaker logistics and a prep call on run-of-show, Q&A and polls.
4. **The webinar.** We run production and manage the audience. Your speakers present.
5. **Within 48 hours.** Follow-up emails go out with the recording and next steps.
6. **Within one week.** You get the performance report, and the session joins your video library.

**Link:** See the full process → `/how-it-works`

*Design note:* A horizontal numbered timeline on desktop, vertical on mobile. Use this same six-step sequence as the backbone of the `/how-it-works` page, expanding each step with the matching items from section 3.
